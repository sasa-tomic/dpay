How it works

Two ways to pay.

Easy. Done in seconds.

Tap to pay

with NFC online or offline

Tap your phone on the shop's phone. Check. Approve. Done. As simple as that. Online or offline, doesn't matter.

1 TapHold your phone on the shop's phone.
2 ApproveConfirm the payment on your phone. no internet needed
3 PaidDone. You both see the transaction.

Scan to pay

with QR

Scan the shop's QR code. Check the amount. Tap Approve. Paid in seconds.

1 Shop's codeThe shop shows a QR code.
2 Scan and approveCheck the amount, then approve.
3 PaidSettled instantly.
Works offline

No internet? One extra scan.

Approve as always. Your phone shows a QR code that confirms the payment. The shop scans it with their phone. Done. You just paid. While offline. It's like magic!

1 ApproveCheck the amount. Tap Approve.
2 Show your codeYour phone shows a code.
3 Shop scansThe shop scans your screen.
4 DoneThe shop sends it for you. Paid.

You check the amount. You approve. The shop has internet. So you don't need any.

For merchants

Take payments in minutes.

No signups, no hardware, no fees. Open the ∂pay wallet on any phone or in any browser, choose Receive, and show your QR. Anyone with the app can pay you.

01 Open the web wallet In any browser, nothing to install. dpay.kalaj.org/wallet.
02 Create your wallet Pick a username, write down your backup words. Your key stays on your device.
03 Receive is your terminal Enter an amount, show the QR. Or share the link. The customer scans and pays.

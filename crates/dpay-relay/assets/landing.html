How it works

Two ways to pay.

Your phone signs every payment offline. The only question is how it reaches the shop.

Tap to pay

It signs the exact amount on contact. The shop's connection settles it. Same three steps, online or off.

Why dpay

Fewer things to go wrong.

A few ways it's different from a card or a bank app.

Online or offline, same payment.

Most wallets need a connection the moment you pay. That's the first thing to fail on a foreign SIM or three floors underground. dpay doesn't. Your phone signs anyway, and the shop's connection carries it through.

No foreign-card fees

You pay in stable currency and the shop gets the same. Nothing goes through a card network to add a cross-border markup.

Only you can move your money

Your key stays on your phone. There's no dpay account in the middle, so we can't hold, freeze, or move your funds.

Held in stable currency. The app shows a $ figure — no token to figure out, no exchange to open.

# dpay — Consumer teaser (30s, vertical 9:16)

*Name, wordmark and slogan are decided, not placeholders: `docs/specs/2026-08-17-name-decision.md`. Money is always shown as `$`; no crypto/ledger jargon on screen. Sound-off friendly: every message lands as on-screen text.*

**Format:** 30s · 9:16 vertical · TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts
**Audience:** everyday consumers — travelers, students, people sending money to family, anyone tired of cards declining or needing a bank
**The wow:** you can pay even when *your* phone has no signal. It settles in seconds. No bank account. Borderless. Your money stays yours.

## Accuracy guardrails (kept true to the product)
- Claim is "pay even when **you** have no signal" — the payer is offline, the receiver submits when online. Never "both sides offline."
- "Settles in seconds," "no bank account needed," "borderless," "your money stays yours" — all consistent with the non-custodial, offline-authorize / online-settle design.
- Amounts shown only as `$`. No mention of the underlying stablecoin or ledger.

## Style
Dark near-black background, one vivid electric accent, money / "Paid ✓" moments in fresh green. Bold confident sans-serif, kinetic typography, fast punchy pacing, premium and trustworthy (not gimmicky).
